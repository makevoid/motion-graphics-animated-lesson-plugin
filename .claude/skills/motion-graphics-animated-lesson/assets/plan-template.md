# Animated lesson production plan

## Brief and production scope

Topic/artifact, audience/prerequisites, learning outcome, duration target, supplied script/reference, 16:9 / 24fps / 1080p or requested format. Planned generation calls/seconds, reuse, pricing evidence/uncertainty, retry allowance and existing authorization. No song required. Include necessary uploads and production across scenes within budget.

## Teaching arc and full narration script

Cold-open mystery/demo, title/host, concepts/examples, complication/peak, synthesis, running-gag payoff. One stable line ID per speaker turn; write every line, pronunciation choices and intended pauses. About 150 words/min is an estimate; measured TTS determines final frames.

## Cast and style bible

Roles needed/omitted and explicit overrides. Professor = George / 0.45 / 11; developer = Liam / 0.5 / 41 when retained. Record sheet paths, no-change identity anchors, hand/prop canon, additional characters and their voices. Preserve source sheets; version intentional replacements. Full keyframe/acting prompts include image references.

Warm cel cartoon style paragraph and palette shared with p5, or user-requested replacement. Character on flat green, still plates for settings, p5 for exact text/diagrams/cameras. Fonts: bundled project filenames, purpose, glyph coverage and any substitutions. Load ending layout PNGs and curtain images as visual references.

## Research

Link `RESEARCH.md`; every on-screen claim/date/quote/number has evidence and a scene use. Distinguish fiction/jokes from facts.

## Scene storyboard

| Scene | Concept/line IDs | Approximate seconds | Acting/gestures | Plate/camera | Diagram/text/captions | Joke/SFX | Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For each scene write image/edit prompts, referenced cast, individual H3 windows and speaker stem, 1080P generated seconds, p5 layers and event cues, card reading time and acceptance criteria. Keep speaking clips at original speed. After TTS write exact `[start,end)` scene frames and section-local audio offsets in `docs/TIMING.md`, without rewriting this plan just for measured timing.

## Music and finish

Reuse original intro/titles by default; select class/blackboard/devroom as appropriate. List measured segment plan, ducking, sparse SFX and intentional silence. Ending: callback → 2 s pause → 1 s iris/tada → folding curtain close/open → source/takeaway card + parade → optional rewind → cast/crew/joke credits → final callback. Reserve narration tail and budget for the entire ending.

Fill `docs/CREDITS.json` from the credits template with actual used cast, voices, model versions, tools, fonts, sources and production attribution. Choose image layouts from `assets/templates/ending/`; original example text is not new-lesson content.

## Production and review

Pilot hook/title + teaching moment, then dependency-ready waves; workers if available, serial otherwise. Immutable references, one owner per RUN, actual request IDs, bounded repair allowance and review evidence. Inspect alpha, identity, drift, lipsync, caption/code spacing, camera bounds, joins, music intelligibility, ending and readable credits. Verify frame equality across narration-only, music and final exports.

## Delivery

Final video, narration-only and music versions, contact sheet, script/plan/research/timing, identity references, templates, prompts, audio masters/stems, manifests and unresolved limitations.
